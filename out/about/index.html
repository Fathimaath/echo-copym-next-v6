ABOUT US

Copym is a leading Real-World Asset tokenization platform designed to transform how individuals invest and manage entitlement. The traditional asset market is fragmented, slow, and geographically restricted.

Democratizing Investment

To make asset entitlement borderless, transparent, and universally accessible through blockchain innovation. We democratize access to entitlement opportunities by enabling fractional entitlement of premium assets, making it possible for anyone to invest in real estate, commodities, and valuable assets with as little as $100.

“A seamless real-world asset ecosystem—transparent chains, responsible custody, sustainable markets.”

— Kuldeep Mahindrkar, CEO, Copym
